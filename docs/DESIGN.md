# Riftwell interface direction

Credit lines, a pooled USDC vault and a marketplace for vote-escrowed NFT positions. Dark ink surfaces with an optional light theme, the selected market’s accent (light green for KittenSwap) and a restrained portal motif. A quiet, premium financial tool—not a neon trading terminal. Typography: crisp display headings, compact legible UI and tabular figures for every amount. The header brand mark is a custom orbit portal.

Information architecture: primary navigation contains exactly Borrow, Earn and Marketplace. Resources—the repayment simulator, statistics, FAQ, keyboard shortcuts, the about/service-status dialog, docs and source—live in a header menu and the footer; the footer also links the brand kit and privacy notes. Keep the market selector and preview or connected status compact. Primary views open directly on balances, positions, forms or listings, with an accessible page heading. Do not add repeated chain/market labels, introductory heroes, release tickers or migration announcements above those controls. Put release notes and explanations in resources or contextual help. Routes are hash-based (`#borrow`, `#earn`, `#marketplace`, `#marketplace/<position>`, `#simulator`, `#stats`, `#faq`, `#brand`, `#privacy`); the former `#lending` route opens Borrow, and any other hash shows a not-found page.

Borrow: vault stat cards with disclosure details, then a tabbed workspace for positions, vote planning and activity. Show collateral credit, borrowed debt, remaining borrowing capacity and wallet balance separately; payoff estimates are labelled examples, not schedules. Deposited collateral, reward-relayer positions and wallet positions are separate lists. The reward relayer collects a position’s rewards each epoch without borrowing; relayer positions never back credit, but a chosen share of their rewards can repay debt. Deposited positions can grow in place: merge a wallet position into one, or add tokens to its lock. Both raise credit without new debt, and each review shows the resulting lock, unlock date and credit first. Vote planning uses example pools and never casts a vote.

Earn: one vault card per market showing vault assets, active loans, utilization and the user’s position. Show share value separately from immediately withdrawable cash. Yield reads “Variable” until the user simulates epochs, and is then labelled simulated. Do not show lender offers or fixed loan rates/durations.

Marketplace: a collection strip (floor ask, best discount, 30-day volume, listed balance), a sortable listings table with discount (and the amount below reference value), locked balance, lock remaining and ask, multi-select sweeps with a sweep slider, public and private (OTC) seller listings with expiry and cancellation, and a paginated sales history. Market stats chart average discount, sales or volume over 7 days, 30 days or all epochs. A purchase can go to the wallet, straight into the credit line (deposit, optional borrow, pay seller) or into the reward relayer, with every step and fee shown before confirmation. Each preview listing has a shareable deep link. Sample sales are labelled as sample data. Connected mode retains fixed and Dutch records, private buyer restrictions, creator pagination, authoritative fresh purchase reviews, and cancellation during chain-read outages. It does not substitute example listings for unavailable data.

Motion and performance: view transitions between routes, short staggered entrances, sliding tab and navigation indicators, count-up figures that always expose the exact value to assistive technology, and toasts. Animate transform and opacity only; reduced motion removes all of it. Secondary pages and dialogs are code-split and loaded while idle so they open without a loading flash. System fonts only.

Production frontend requirements: responsive down to 320px; no horizontal overflow; keyboard accessible inputs, navigation, tabs, menus and dialogs; dialog focus containment, Escape dismissal and focus restoration; visible focus; reduced-motion support; semantic labels and appropriate contrast in both themes; no external trackers, fonts or media; no console errors; strict TypeScript; realistic loading, empty, error and filtered-empty states; no dead buttons.

Preview mode is clearly labelled and uses illustrative data. Connected mode signs wallet authentication messages and reads confirmed ownership, but never requests token approval, NFT transfers or contract transactions. A meaningful simulated workflow may use the browser’s local storage for a sample portfolio and explicitly show its preview status. No fake public live metrics. Portal visuals stay decorative and never block actions.

Use semantic headings, labels and tables; visible focus; keyboard navigation; contained dialog focus, Escape dismissal and focus restoration; and reduced-motion support. No external trackers, fonts or decorative media dependencies. Keep page width contained down to 320px.

Content tone: short, calm, useful. No competitors, private collaborators, personal wallets, or private research in the public repository. No inflated "10/10" claims, unsupported security claims or AI marketing boilerplate. Use the name Riftwell consistently.

Source layout: `src/app/` holds routing, theme, motion, toasts, shortcuts and lazy loading; `src/components/` holds the shell, dialogs and UI primitives; `src/pages/` holds the preview pages; `src/connected/` holds connected mode; `src/styles/` holds tokens, base, shell, component and page styles. Preview accounting stays in `src/lending.ts`, `src/listings.ts`, `src/vote.ts` and `src/simulator.ts` with unit tests beside them.
