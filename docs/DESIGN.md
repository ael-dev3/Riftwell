# Riftwell interface direction

Credit lines, a pooled USDC vault and a marketplace for vote-escrowed NFT positions. Dark ink surfaces with an optional light theme, the selected market’s accent (light green for KittenSwap) and a restrained portal motif. A quiet, premium financial tool—not a neon trading terminal. Typography: crisp display headings, compact legible UI and tabular figures for every amount. The header brand mark is a custom orbit portal.

Information architecture: primary navigation contains exactly Borrow, Earn and Marketplace. Resources—the repayment simulator, FAQ, keyboard shortcuts, the about/service-status dialog and source—live in a header menu and the footer, not in more navigation sections. A context bar under the header shows the page, chain, market selector, the market’s epoch countdown and the preview or connected status. Routes are hash-based (`#borrow`, `#earn`, `#marketplace`, `#marketplace/<position>`, `#simulator`, `#faq`); the former `#lending` route opens Borrow.

Borrow: vault stat cards with disclosure details, then a tabbed workspace for positions, vote planning and activity. Show collateral credit, borrowed debt, remaining borrowing capacity and wallet balance separately; payoff estimates are labelled examples, not schedules. Deposited collateral and wallet positions are separate lists. Vote planning uses example pools and never casts a vote.

Earn: one vault card per market showing vault assets, active loans, utilization and the user’s position. Show share value separately from immediately withdrawable cash. Yield reads “Variable” until the user simulates epochs, and is then labelled simulated. Do not show lender offers or fixed loan rates/durations.

Marketplace: a collection strip (floor ask, discounts, listed balance), a sortable listings table with discount, locked balance, lock remaining and ask, multi-select sweeps, seller listings with expiry and cancellation, and history. A purchase can go to the wallet or straight into the credit line (deposit, optional borrow, pay seller), with every step and fee shown before confirmation. Each listing has a shareable deep link.

Motion and performance: view transitions between routes, short staggered entrances, sliding tab and navigation indicators, count-up figures that always expose the exact value to assistive technology, and toasts. Animate transform and opacity only; reduced motion removes all of it. Secondary pages and dialogs are code-split and loaded while idle so they open without a loading flash. System fonts only.

Production frontend requirements: responsive down to 320px; no horizontal overflow; keyboard accessible inputs, navigation, tabs, menus and dialogs; dialog focus containment, Escape dismissal and focus restoration; visible focus; reduced-motion support; semantic labels and appropriate contrast in both themes; no external trackers, fonts or media; no console errors; strict TypeScript; realistic loading, empty, error and filtered-empty states; no dead buttons.

The interface is a clearly labelled preview. Data are illustrative. No wallet transactions, signatures, token approval or contract deployment. A meaningful simulated workflow may use the browser’s local storage for a sample portfolio and explicitly show its preview status. No fake public live metrics. Portal visuals stay decorative and never block actions.

Fee policy displayed in action reviews and receipts: marketplace seller pays 0.5% of sale price; borrowing pays a one-time 0.5% origination fee. Configured revenue shares and any future automation/vault fees are separate from origination. No reward fee schedule is assumed for an undeployed vault. No guarantees or invented live yields. Collateral examples are explicitly labelled demo veKITTEN positions with illustrative KITTEN balances. The supplied KittenSwap logo identifies the only current market; original portal artwork remains decorative.

Content tone: short, calm, useful. No competitors, private collaborators, personal wallets, or private research in the public repository. No inflated "10/10" claims, unsupported security claims or AI marketing boilerplate. Use the name Riftwell consistently.

Source layout: `src/app/` holds routing, theme, motion, toasts, shortcuts and lazy loading; `src/components/` holds the shell, dialogs and UI primitives; `src/pages/` holds the preview pages; `src/connected/` holds connected mode; `src/styles/` holds tokens, base, shell, component and page styles. Preview accounting stays in `src/lending.ts`, `src/listings.ts`, `src/vote.ts` and `src/simulator.ts` with unit tests beside them.
