# Riftwell interface direction

Two main sections: Lending and Marketplace. Dark surfaces, KittenSwap's light-green accent and a quiet portal mark. The selected market is visible in the header. Keep copy short and controls explicit.

The marketplace opens directly on a listings table with List yours, Buy and sweep controls. Show token ID, locked balance, lock date, ask and unit price together. All listings, My listings and History share that surface. Seller edits and cancellation belong next to the owned record. On narrow screens, the table scrolls within its container while row actions remain visible. No promotional hero, decorative NFT gallery, fake discounts or unsupported volume statistics.

Lending separates collateral, debt and available credit from pooled USDC supply and withdrawable vault cash. Supporting details live in accessible dialogs. Do not introduce request/offer matching, fixed loan maturities or invented live APR into the pooled product.

Preview data and balances are clearly illustrative. Connected mode uses confirmed ownership reads and persistent off-chain records, with loading, empty and unavailable states. It never substitutes demo data after a service failure. Fees belong in action reviews; seller fees are 0.5% of marketplace sale price. No undeployed lending fee schedule is assumed.

Use semantic headings, labels and tables; visible focus; keyboard navigation; contained dialog focus, Escape dismissal and focus restoration; and reduced-motion support. No external trackers, fonts or decorative media dependencies. Keep page width contained down to 320px.

Product language stays calm, concise and useful. No private research, counterparties, personal wallets, inflated quality claims or marketing boilerplate in the public repository. Original code and artwork remain distinct from supplied collection branding.
